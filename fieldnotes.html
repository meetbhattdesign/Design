Observations on the UX design world Curated · Updated periodically
Trend · AI Design
AI is not replacing designers - it's raising the bar for craft
Tools like Figma AI, Galileo, and Uizard are handling low-level execution. The real designer's job is now sharper: better taste, better systems thinking, better questions.
Nielsen Norman Group · 2026
Research · Interaction
Spatial computing is redrawing the rules of interface design
With Apple Vision Pro in the wild, designers are unlearning flat-screen instincts. Depth, gaze, and gesture are now first-class design inputs - not edge cases.
UX Collective · 2026
Insight · Accessibility
Accessible design is finally being treated as strategy, not compliance
WCAG 3.0 is shifting from pass/fail checklists to outcome-based scoring. Teams that baked accessibility into their design systems early are now ahead.
A11y Project · 2026
Opinion · Process
The best UX research is happening outside the lab
Guerrilla testing, diary studies, and contextual inquiry are outperforming moderated sessions. Real contexts generate better insight than controlled ones.
Smashing Magazine · 2026
Method · Systems
Design tokens are becoming the single source of truth for cross-platform teams
From Figma variables to code-level tokens, more teams are treating design decisions as data. This is shrinking the designer-developer handoff from days to minutes.
Figma Blog · 2026
Shift · User Behaviour
Users are developing AI fatigue - simplicity is the new premium
After years of feature overload, users are gravitating toward products that do one thing extremely well. Less surface area, more trust.
NN/g Research · 2026
Practice · Research
Continuous discovery is replacing quarterly research sprints
Product teams are embedding lightweight user touchpoints - weekly interviews, session recordings, feedback loops - instead of big-bang usability studies every quarter.
Teresa Torres · Continuous Discovery · 2026
Craft · Typography
Variable fonts are finally going mainstream in product design
With broader browser support and Figma's variable font panel, designers are using weight and width axes for responsive type systems instead of loading multiple weights.
Google Fonts · 2026
Industry · Hiring
Portfolios with working prototypes outperform case study decks in hiring
Design hiring managers report that interactive, shipped, or coded work demonstrates capability more convincingly than polished case study slides - especially at mid-level.
Linkedin Design Community · 2026
