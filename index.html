I'm Meet - a product designer based in Pune, India. B.Des in Interaction Design, Anant National University (2026). My capstone thesis was EKON - an ML platform for individual bird identification.

My practice spans UX-UI design, VR/AR, branding, and cinematography. I've worked on conservation data tools, spatial experiences, brand systems, and mobile products. I research carefully and design with intent.

Outside the studio I document birds and wildlife across India - Gujarat, Maharashtra, and beyond. Field observation and design share one rule: look before you act.
