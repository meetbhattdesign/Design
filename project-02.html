PETCO.

Pet Care App · UX/UI Design · Mobile

Designed a comprehensive pet care platform that connects pet parents with trusted veterinary services, NGO rescue networks, and essential pet resources - all in one unified, empathy-driven app.

01Project Overview

One app for every pet parent.

Petco is a mobile application designed to simplify and unify pet care in India. The fragmented landscape of vet directories, adoption portals, pet shops, and NGO websites forces pet parents to manage care across multiple disconnected platforms.

This project set out to replace that friction with a single, empathetic product that handles the full spectrum of pet parenthood - from routine vaccination reminders to emergency rescue coordination.

The Problem Space
Pet parents in India navigate multiple disconnected platforms for vet bookings, NGO support, adoption, and pet supplies - leading to missed care, delayed rescues, and poor health outcomes for animals.
The Design Goal
Build a unified, empathy-first platform that reduces friction at every step of pet care - from routine checkups to emergency rescue - while supporting the broader animal welfare ecosystem.
02Problem Statement

Fragmented care is failing pets and their parents.

Pet parents face a fragmented ecosystem - vet directories, adoption portals, rescue NGOs, and pet supply stores all exist in isolation. There is no unified platform that lets users book vet consultations, connect with NGOs for rescue or adoption, and access quality pet accessories in one place.

This fragmentation leads to delayed medical care, missed adoption opportunities, and a lack of community support for stray animal welfare.

How Might We
"How might we design a unified platform that empowers pet parents to manage healthcare, adoption, rescue, and supplies - while supporting the wider animal welfare ecosystem?"
03Research

Understanding pet parents and their pain points.

I conducted user interviews and competitive analysis across existing pet care platforms to identify where the experience breaks down. Key pain points emerged consistently across user types.

Key Pain Points
No single place to book online and offline vet consultations.

NGO and rescue information is scattered and hard to verify.

Vaccination and medication tracking is manual and error-prone.

Lost pet reporting has no coordinated community system.
User Types Identified
New pet parent - overwhelmed, needs guidance and trusted vet access.

Experienced pet parent - needs efficiency, health records, and reminders.

Animal welfare advocate - needs tools to coordinate rescues and donations.
04Solution

A unified platform built with empathy.

The Petco app integrates veterinary services, NGO support, and pet care resources into a single, intuitive experience. Every feature was designed around reducing friction and building trust - for both pet parents and the animals they care for.

01
Vet Consultations
Book online video consultations or in-clinic appointments with verified vets. Filter by specialty, location, and availability.
02
NGO & Rescue Support
Connect with nearby verified NGOs for rescue coordination, emergency animal care, and volunteer opportunities.
03
Adoption & Foster
Browse adoptable animals from verified shelters and NGOs. Start fostering journeys with guided onboarding and support.
04
Pet Health Records
Maintain a digital health passport for each pet - vaccination history, vet visit logs, medications, and growth tracking.
05
Pet Accessories Marketplace
Curated marketplace for trusted pet food, accessories, and healthcare products - with vet-recommended tags.
06
Stray Care & Community Alerts
Report injured strays, share location-tagged alerts, and coordinate community response through a live feed.
07
Vaccination & Medication Reminders
Smart reminders based on each pet's health records. Never miss a booster or monthly medication dose.
08
Lost & Found Tracking
Post lost pet reports with photos and last seen location. Community-powered found pet matching with push notifications.
09
Volunteer & Donation Portal
Support verified NGOs through transparent donation flows and sign up for local volunteering shifts directly in-app.
05Design

Simplicity and empathy at every touchpoint.

The visual design language was built around warmth, clarity, and trust. Pet care is an emotional domain - the app needed to feel reassuring under stress (emergency rescue) and delightful during everyday moments (tracking your pet's growth).

01
Empathy first
Every flow was designed around the emotional state of the user - anxious during emergencies, excited during adoption, routine during health tracking.
02
Trust through verification
Verified badges on vets, NGOs, and sellers. Users should never have to wonder if they are engaging with a legitimate service.
03
Progressive complexity
New pet parents get guided flows. Experienced users get quick access to records, history, and advanced filters - without cluttering the core experience.
04
Community as infrastructure
Lost pets, stray alerts, and rescue coordination are community features - designed to work through collective action, not just individual use.

06Outcome & Learnings

What this project taught me.

Petco was a 25-day design sprint that pushed me to balance breadth with coherence - nine features, three user types, one unified experience. The constraint of time made every design decision count.

01
Emotional context shapes interaction design. Pet care is high-stakes emotionally. Designing for both the anxious emergency state and the calm daily routine required distinct tonal registers within the same app.
02
Feature breadth demands ruthless prioritisation. With nine features, the temptation is to give each equal weight. The real work was identifying the three flows that needed to be flawless - and making those the backbone of the app.
03
Community features are UX problems, not just product problems. Lost pet tracking and stray alerts only work when users actually contribute. Designing for participation - not just consumption - was a new design challenge for me.
